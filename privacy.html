Privacy Policy

Keep My Lotto · Effective date: July 16, 2026 · Last updated: July 16, 2026
Short version: Keep My Lotto stores everything you enter on your own device. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information, and we have no accounts or logins. The only data that leaves your device is an anonymous request to a public lottery-results service to fetch winning numbers.

1. Who we are

Keep My Lotto (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal record-keeping tool that lets you log lottery tickets you have already purchased, check them against official results, and keep a spending record. The app does not sell tickets, run drawings, accept wagers, or handle money. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Powerball®, Mega Millions®, or any lottery operator.

2. Information we store on your device

The following is saved locally on your device (using the operating system’s secure key-value storage) and is never sent to us:

  • Ticket entries you create: game, draw date, numbers, cost, and options.
  • Saved favorite number sets.
  • Any winning numbers you enter manually, and a cached copy of results fetched from the public results service.
  • Your app settings and preferences.

Because this data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes it. We cannot recover it for you.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or account — the app has no sign-in.
  • No advertising identifiers used to build a profile of you by us.
  • No location data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. We do not sell your data.

4. Network requests

To show winning numbers, the app requests results from a public, official open-data service (New York State Open Data, published by the NY Lottery). These requests contain only what is needed to retrieve results (for example, the game and draw date) and do not include your ticket numbers or any information that identifies you.

5. Advertising

The app is supported by advertising. Ads may be provided by a third-party ad network, which may use a device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads in accordance with its own policies and your device settings. We configure the ad network to exclude gambling-category advertisements. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device’s privacy settings. [If you use a specific network such as Google AdMob, name it here and link its policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads]

6. Children

The app concerns lottery play and is intended for adults only (18+, or 21+ where required). It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Data security and retention

Your entries are stored using your device’s protected storage and are retained until you delete them or uninstall the app. Because we do not receive your data, we do not retain it on any server.

8. Your choices

  • Delete individual tickets or favorites in the app at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Manage ad personalization through your device’s advertising settings.

9. Not proof of purchase; verify results

Entries in the app are a personal record and are not proof of purchase. Your physical printed ticket is the only valid proof of a prize. Winning numbers and any payout estimates shown may be delayed or incorrect; always verify with the official lottery before acting.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@keepmylotto.com.
